I am an undergraduate researcher pursuing a B.A. in Psychology with Honors at the University of California, Riverside, with graduation expected in June 2027. My research examines cognitive load, comprehension, flexible transfer, and the use of generative AI in learning.

I aim to evaluate educational technologies that help learners retain and use knowledge while maintaining agency over the learning process. My work includes independent research at UC Riverside and a research assistantship with Stanford University's DISTAL Lab.
