I am an undergraduate researcher pursuing a B.A. in Psychology with Honors at the University of California, Riverside, with graduation expected in June 2027.

My current objective is to design, develop, and evaluate the effectiveness of educational technologies and learning scaffolds that aim to expand educational accessibility and equity. In doing so, I aim to equip all learners with high utility tools they can harness to take agency over their learning, improve their learning efficiency, and effectively retain and utilize their knowledge in innovative ways.

I am currently applying to interdisciplinary IS and CS PhD programs for Fall 2027 admission to extend my current research and work toward my objective.
